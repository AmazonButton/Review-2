The 5 most important findings:

1. **SR-01:** Align the trusted payment redirect policy with the intended staging origin and test the real payment-link request without broadening it to arbitrary hosts.
2. **SR-02:** Specify and implement the rental-specific provider/activation/return lifecycle, retaining explicit guards and terminal outcomes.
3. **SR-03:** Reconcile shift closure and handover rules, then verify that an OPEN shift cannot bypass required statistics finalization.
4. **SR-04:** Align specialized store-role navigation and per-operation permissions across Web and Backend, with actor-specific negative tests.
5. **SR-05:** Rerun the affected bank/DLQ suites on the corrective source and isolated dependencies; selected cancellation CI is insufficient to close those findings.
